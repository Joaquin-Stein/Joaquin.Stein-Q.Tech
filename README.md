## Dataset

- **Source:** `QforMedia_TakeHome_Data.xlsx` — two sheets (Video Daily Performance, AB Tests)
- **Scope:** 10 videos × 10 days = 100 rows · 5 A/B thumbnail tests
- **Tool:** Python · pandas · Chart.js

## Key findings

1. Science & Future Tech leads on CTR (4.76%) — Engineering & Construction leads on retention (47.9%)
2. Hyperloop and World's Largest 3D Printer are the channel's strongest all-round performers
3. Building an Underground House has the joint-highest retention (51%) but near-zero algorithmic visibility — a discoverability problem, not a content problem
